Draft Battle VS ("the Service") is a game where people draft items from topics, post results, and vote. This policy explains what we collect and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Account data: email address, password (stored hashed by our authentication provider, never in plain text), username, optional bio and profile picture, and your friend code.
  • Guest data: if you continue as a guest, we create an anonymous ID and a random guest name. No email is collected.
  • Content you create: topics and items, posted drafts (topic, players, picks, round order), and your friends list.
  • Votes: which team you voted for in each draft. Your individual votes are private. Only totals and percentages are shown.
  • Live-room data: your display name and in-draft activity are shared with others in the same room while you play.
  • Technical data: our hosting providers may log IP addresses and device information for security and operation. A session token is stored in your browser to keep you signed in.

What is public

Your username, profile picture, bio, rankings, number of drafts and votes received, public topics, and drafts you post to the gallery can be seen by anyone. Private topics are visible only to you and people who have the topic code. Your email is never shown publicly.

How we use data

To run accounts, rooms, the gallery, voting and rankings; to prevent cheating and abuse; to moderate content; and to keep the Service secure. We do not sell your data and do not run advertising or analytics trackers.

Moderation

Topics, items, usernames and bios are checked automatically for prohibited language, and content may be removed. Accounts that abuse the Service may be restricted or deleted.

Service providers

We use Supabase (database, authentication, live rooms) and GitHub Pages (website hosting). They process data on our behalf under their own privacy terms.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. To delete your account and associated content, email [CONTACT EMAIL]. Guest data may be removed after a period of inactivity.

Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use access rules, encrypted connections and hashed passwords. No system is perfectly secure.
